Strategy

Also known as an "Executive Purpose Playbook" or "Personal Brand Strategy." This is like a brand social media playbook, but specifically for just one individual. For example, a brand executive or spokesperson, consultant, small business owner, or anyone seeking to optimize their digital presence. The strategy may include:

  • Personal and professional purpose statements
  • Personal brand elements
  • Content and thought leadership pillars
  • Content production and publishing cadence
  • Key digital channels
  • Integrations with other marketing plans (e.g., PR, internal and external comms)

A Playbook is a complete system outlining every component of a brand's social media strategy and operations. Each component and nuance is tied directly to business, marketing, and social media objectives, ensuring team certainty and alignment.
