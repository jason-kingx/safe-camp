Hello Mike, Ben, Raffi, and the Mozilla team —
These materials were created to share a glimpse of how I think, and how I'm thinking about this project opportunity.
Overview

Mozilla is not your average tech company. I am not your average social media marketer.
I have specialized in social media and community for 15 years, working with more than 100 brands through internal, agency, and startup roles — independent consulting too.

As a result of so many different experiences, I bring a critical lens and my own points of view.
I view social media as a finite system, like a sports play or a car engine.
Every component can be decided, documented, and optimized.
This is the difference between making content from vibes and chasing vanity metrics, versus using social media to drive real business results.

Community can be defined, visualized, and actioned in the same way.
It is a practice and an ideal — a crucial part of the "social" in social media.
With this lens, my past work has engaged Mozilla's key audiences: developers, users, policymakers, and communities.

The structure of social media and community strategy parallels the open-source AI stack.

Open-source contributors have two jobs — to write code, and be easy to work with.
My job has always been: to do good work, and be easy to work with.
I would like to work with the Mozilla team, to shape the future of intelligence.
