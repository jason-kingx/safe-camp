Social media is a dark
and noisy forest.

Full of channels, features, search results,
news, and opinions. And those algorithms…

So it's no wonder that brands get
distracted and lost in the wilderness.

The good thing is that it
doesn't have to be this way.

Strategy is what clears
a path through the darkness.

Strategy is a finite system
with specific components.

These include the operations and outcomes of content, community management, advertising, and more.

Strategy must connect each component to business,
marketing, and social media objectives.

With evidence that they will drive business growth, not vanity metrics.

Strategy connects every decision,
workflow, and stakeholder.

This ensures the team is aligned and heading in the same direction.

In the dark and noisy forest, a system is

Strategy as shelter.
