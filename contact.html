Little Answers FAQ

Who is this for?

Marketers and communications professionals. Big and small businesses. Anyone who uses social media to drive business results. And anyone who is curious about social media.

How many questions can I ask?

Please include one focused question per form submission. If you include multiple questions, I will have to make sense of your submission and answer only one question. If you ask a follow-up question, together we can arrange another form submission or determine what will best meet your needs.

What kinds of questions can I ask?

Anything related to social media, that will be helpful to you. For example, questions about strategy, platforms, content, tactics, etc. If you ask me who won the latest football game, I probably won't know!

When will my question be answered?

Questions are typically answered within 24 - 48 hours. I strive to give a thoughtful and thorough answer.

How are questions used?

I ask that you do not include sensitive data, customer information, or similar information. The Little Answers question form only collects an email address and a question. This information is not stored in any database or third-party system — it is sent directly to my inbox via a mailto link. The email address may be retained in that inbox to cross-reference against a Stripe payment record and to send a reply. It will not be used for marketing or added to any mailing list without explicit consent. Submitted questions also help me decide what to write or make content about.
