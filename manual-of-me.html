⚙️ Module 04 How I work
🕐 Time & rhythm
  • Standard hours, with flexibility built in
  • Creative and deep work vary by time of day
  • Protected focus time is important
  • I batch meetings to guard uninterrupted stretches
  • I disconnect after hours, on weekends, and holidays
🪴 Environment
  • Clean, minimal workspace — low stimulus
  • Darker, quiet room helps me focus
  • Lofi music with noise-cancelling headphones
  • Remote work is where I do my best work
📋 Tools & habits
  • Calendar planning — I think in weeks
  • To-do lists keep me grounded
  • I write processes to create shared clarity and prevent mismatched expectations
  • Autonomy + thought-partnership over either alone
🔁 How I approach projects
  • I work best when we align on an MVP and timeline first — it keeps the scope from becoming open-ended
  • I go deep, then share for a gut check, feedback, and revision
  • I cover bases thoroughly, but I'm actively working against the pull to perfect everything before shipping
  • Asking me a question — big or small — can spark an idea or get me unstuck
💬 Module 05 Communication style

I keep Slack and email open during the day — Slack is where I respond fastest. My core hours are roughly 9am–5pm, and I snooze work notifications from 7pm to 7am. Async is my default, but I'm genuinely happy to jump on a call anytime — please don't hesitate.

On feedback

In the moment, directly, and 1:1 whenever possible — not relayed through someone else, especially if it's critical. I'd rather hear it straight from you. Pair it with specific context so I understand not just what, but why.

Things people sometimes get wrong
  • My thoughtfulness can look like hesitation or indecisiveness — sometimes I just need a minute, an hour, or a day to think it through.
  • Being calm and quiet can read as aloof, disinterested, or opinionless. The reality is simpler: I'm calmly observing, processing, thinking.
  • If I go quiet or slow to respond, I'm likely overwhelmed — not disengaged. A gentle check-in helps more than you'd expect.
  • Once you know me, I'm funnier and sillier than my professional or strategy-focused presence might suggest.
⚡ Module 06 What supercharges me & what drains me
What supercharges me
  • Clear context and goals, with freedom in how to get there
  • A balance of autonomy and thought-partnership
  • Working remotely
  • Solving problems that genuinely matter
  • Collaborating with people who are as invested as I am
  • When someone tells me we're on track — I have a hard time giving myself permission to slow down, and that nudge matters
What drains me
  • Micromanagement
  • Meetings without clear purpose or outcomes
  • Being excluded from decisions that affect my work
  • Feeling like my input isn't heard or valued
  • Environments with negativity or gossip
  • When I'm overwhelmed, my stress response is to freeze — I go quiet and slow down, not act out. If that happens, a gentle check-in is the most helpful thing you can do
🤝 Module 07 What you can count on me for & what I need
You can count on me for
  • Showing up prepared and on time
  • Bringing a thoughtful, human perspective
  • Following through on what I commit to
  • Intrinsic motivation — I don't need to be pushed
  • Distilling complexity into strategy, systems, and operations
  • Listening actively and creating safe space for others
What I need from you
  • Clear communication and follow-through
  • Loop me in early, not at the last minute
  • Space to think before responding
  • Trust and autonomy once context is set
  • Honest, direct feedback
  • Collaborative energy, not just task delegation
  • Tell me when we're on track — I tend to push through without stopping, and knowing things are good gives me permission to actually rest
